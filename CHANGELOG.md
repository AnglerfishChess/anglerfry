# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-31

First release: a small, readable UCI chess engine to build your own on top
of. A stdin loop that keeps answering while a search thread thinks; two
strategies behind the `Strategy` option (`random`, `two-ply`); time, depth,
node, mate and `searchmoves` limits; clean conformance on uci-test-suite
levels L0–L3 and L5–L6.
